**For Dative Plurals, remember to add an -n to the end of the noun.**  
**z. B: die Äpfel → den Äpfeln**  

**Exceptions to the -n rule:**
- Nouns already ending in -n (die Frauen → den Frauen)
- Nouns ending in -s (die Autos → den Autos)
