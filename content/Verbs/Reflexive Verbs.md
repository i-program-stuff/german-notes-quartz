Reflexive verbs are verbs where you are the object and subject of the action.

- Example in English: I wash myself.  
- Same sentence in German: Ich wasche mich.

| Person    | Reflexive Pronoun |
| --------- | ----------------- |
| ich       | **mich**          |
| du        | **dich**          |
| er/sie/es | **sich**          |
| wir       | **uns**           |
| ihr       | **euch**          |
| sie/Sie   | **sich**          |

- Ich treffe mich mit ihm. → I meet with him (This specifically means a planned meet up with a person.)
- Ich treffe ihn → I meet him.
- Wir treffen uns mit ihnen. → We meet with them.

If there's another object you can use dative.
- Ich wasche mir die Hände. → I wash my hands.
- Sie kämmt ihr die Haare.  → She is combing her hair.

#### Common Reflexive Verbs
| Verb                         | Meaning                  |
| ---------------------------- | ------------------------ |
| **sich waschen**             | to wash oneself          |
| **sich anziehen**            | to get dressed           |
| **sich freuen**              | to be happy (about sth.) |
| **sich erinnern (an)**       | to remember              |
| **sich setzen**              | to sit down              |
| **sich interessieren (für)** | to be interested (in)    |
| **sich treffen (mit)**       | to meet (with)           |
| **sich fühlen**              | to feel                  |
